Pet Thermoplastic Splint — Product Overview

A practical, clinic-ready support tool for canine and feline patients. ZHIVON™'s Pet Thermoplastic Splint is intended to aid immobilization and rehabilitation during muscle strains, soft-tissue contusions, and the healing phase of fractures in dogs and cats, and can also provide supportive bracing across spinal, rib, and tail regions. This product is not a medical device.

The range offers four sizes across two formats, so a practice can stock exactly what its daily casework calls for.

Universal · 60 × 100 mm · $15 / sheet

A compact, all-purpose size for smaller dogs and most cats. Suits general-practice clinics handling routine limb support, light strapping, and short-term stabilization. Easy to keep on the shelf as a standard consumable.

Large · 80 × 120 mm · $19 / sheet

An extended footprint for medium-to-large breeds and broader wrapping needs. Works well in general clinics that see a mix of patient sizes and prefer one larger sheet for versatile limb, rib, or tail support.

Professional · 60 × 100 mm · $19 / sheet

Built for in-hospital use where a licensed veterinarian shapes and fits the splint on the animal. Carries a low-sensitivity medical-grade adhesive backing and a bitter-tasting anti-chew coating. Best matched to referral clinics and specialty hospitals managing post-operative or fracture-recovery cases that call for precise, clinician-fitted support.

Professional Large · 80 × 120 mm · $24 / sheet

The larger Professional format for the same in-hospital, vet-fitted workflow. Gives a broader surface for spinal, rib-cage, or larger-breed limb applications that need extra coverage. Adhesive and anti-chew coating are identical to the standard Professional line.

General Notes — Material, Shaping, Wear & Removal

Every sheet is made from a low-temperature thermoplastic polymer and is punched with ventilation holes across the board face for air circulation during wear.

Shaping. Submerge the sheet in hot water at approximately 70 °C until the polymer softens. Lift it out, blot the surface, and conform it to the patient's anatomy. The sheet sets in roughly five minutes as it cools. Should a minor repositioning be needed after it has hardened, a brief, localized re-warm will re-soften just that spot.

Wearing & removal. Each sheet is designed for up to 7 days of wear; the attending veterinarian sets the wearing schedule for each patient. Ring-style wraps closed around a limb can be taken off by gently reheating the polymer until the ring loosens, then sliding it free. The Professional flat-fit sheets, which hold through the medical-grade adhesive backing, are lifted away with a standard adhesive remover; no forced peeling is needed, which keeps the process comfortable for the animal.

All four formats share the same breathability, the same low-temperature working window, and the same "adjust-after-set" convenience. The Professional line simply adds the two in-hospital features — low-allergen backing and bitter coating — for patients that may chew or show skin sensitivity.

Whether your practice is a single-doctor clinic keeping a small stock of Universal sheets or a multi-station hospital running Professional fits every day, the range is built to slot into your existing after-care routine without extra equipment.
