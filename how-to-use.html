Application Guide — Pet Thermoplastic Splint

This guide is prepared for veterinary clinics, animal hospitals, and authorised distributors handling ZHIVON™ Pet Thermoplastic Splints. It outlines the standard shaping and fitting procedure across all four sizes (Universal, Large, Professional, Professional Large). The splint is a non-medical-device support aid intended for assisted immobilisation and rehabilitation support in dogs and cats; it provides mechanical support only and is not a substitute for veterinary diagnosis or care.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Prepare the water. Fill a shallow, heat-resistant basin with water at approximately 70 °C. A simple kitchen thermometer or a dedicated pet-supplies water heater is recommended so the temperature can be confirmed before the piece goes in.
  2. Soak to soften. Submerge the splint piece — formed from a low-temperature thermoplastic polymer — completely in the warm water. Hold it until the material becomes uniformly pliable. A gentle flex test between thumb and forefinger confirms that the piece is ready to shape.
  3. Shape and apply. Lift the softened piece out, shake off excess liquid, and press it against the target area (limb, spine, rib cage, or tail) under the veterinarian's guidance. Smooth the surface, seat the ventilation holes so air can circulate freely, and adjust the contour to the animal's anatomy. For Professional and Professional Large pieces, confirm the low-sensitivity medical adhesive backing is in full contact with the skin and the bitter anti-chew coating faces outward before the piece is secured.
  4. Cool and set. Allow the piece to cool for approximately five minutes. During this window the polymer returns to a firm, stable shape. All contouring and positioning must be completed before the material sets.
  5. Verify the fit. Once set, check that no edge presses into the skin, that ventilation holes remain unobstructed, and that the piece stays in place during mild movement. If a minor correction is needed after setting, a brief, gentle re-heating will permit a small re-shape.

Precautions and reminders

  • Water temperature. Keep the water near 70 °C. Avoid significantly higher temperatures that could scorch the animal's skin or the operator's hands.
  • Timing. All shaping and positioning must be finished before the cooling period ends. Once the polymer has set, further changes require re-heating.
  • Professional-series fitting. Professional and Professional Large splints are designed to be shaped and applied inside a veterinary clinic by a licensed veterinarian. Distributors should not attempt on-site fitting without veterinary supervision.
  • Monitoring. The wearing schedule follows the treating veterinarian's instructions. If the animal shows signs of discomfort, skin redness, or persistent attempts to remove the piece, take it off promptly and contact the clinic.
  • Ventilation. Keep the perforations clear of fur, bandage, or adhesive residue throughout the wearing period.

This splint is a support and positioning aid only. It is not a medical device and does not replace veterinary diagnosis or care.
